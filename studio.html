Studio

A playground for light, glass and type. Everything here is built from scratch in the browser, so move, swipe and click: each piece reacts.

Move to tilt the word and stir the light

01

Glass typeshader · sdf

Type anything and pick a font. The letters are turned into a distance field and wrapped into glass in a shader: thin fonts grow into round tubes, heavy ones puff up like balloons. Light bends through them, splits into colour and catches star-shaped glints.

Press Play to write it on. A inflates the letters one by one. B works out strokes from the letter shapes (thinning them to a skeleton, then ordering the pieces like a hand would), so it works with any font. C writes with real stroke order: a single-line hand drawn for this page, and real stroke order for Chinese. D uses ready-made stroke templates for four script fonts, so the writing joins up like real cursive.

Swipe across the light
02

Light pushfluid

A hidden fluid simulation bends slanted beams of light instead of drawing smoke: swipe and the rays smear, fringe into red, green and blue, and leave a glow that slowly settles. Pick a palette, or mix your own sky, horizon and light.

Technique: Jos Stam’s Stable Fluids (1999), used here only to displace the image.

Click to replay · hover a line
03

Text scrambledom

Letters arrive as coloured noise and settle one by one, line after line.

A terminal-era trope; Justin Windle’s Text Scramble on CodePen is the classic web reference.

Swipe quickly
04

Fluid dyefluid

The same solver, this time pouring coloured light into the flow: it curls, spreads and pushes the type behind it apart.

Pavel Dobryakov’s open-source WebGL Fluid Simulation made this look popular on the web.

Move the sun · hide it behind a cloud or the hills
05

Lens flareshader

A summer sky with the sun on your cursor. The lens answers with a warm glare, six thin aperture spikes, a chain of tinted ghosts through the centre, a faint rainbow halo and specks of dust on the glass. Slide the sun behind a cloud or the treeline and the whole flare fades.

It imitates what real lenses do when they are pointed at something bright.

Quiet tools for loud ideas. Move near the letters
06

Variable typefont axes

Instrument Sans has weight and width axes: letters near the cursor swell, widen and turn amber. Leave, and a slow wave runs through the line.

Variable fonts have worked in every major browser since 2018.

Your cursor is a lamp · click for a ripple
07

ASCII lightcanvas

A moving field of light, redrawn as characters: the brighter it is, the denser the glyph. The cursor is a lamp and a click sends out a ripple.

A nod to the ASCII art of early computers.

A nod to the transitions of early video games and TV.
